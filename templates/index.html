How It Works

A transparent, five-stage NLP pipeline.

01

Enter Text

Paste a sentence, review, or social post into the analyzer.

02

Backend Processing

Flask validates and cleans the input on the server.

03

NLP Scoring

VADER scores the text for positive, negative, neutral, and compound polarity.

04

Classification

The compound score maps to Positive, Negative, or Neutral using standard thresholds.

05

Visualization

Scores, intensity, and text statistics render as charts and a gauge.
